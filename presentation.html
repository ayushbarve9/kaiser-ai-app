Problem Statement

The Broken Urban Grievance Loop

Why traditional municipal complaint portals fail to deliver timely civic repairs

🚫 Spam & Fake Uploads (35%+)
Citizens upload selfies, internet memes, IDE screenshots, and AI-generated fake fixed road photos, overwhelming triage staff.
⏳ Manual Routing Delays (48h+)
Complaints sit in general queues before operators manually assign them to the correct local administrative ward.
👁️ Zero Citizen Transparency
Citizens receive no live status or visual proof of repair, leading to repeated duplicate submissions.
💸 Ghost Repairs & Fraud
Tickets are prematurely marked "Resolved" without verifiable camera sensor timestamps or photographic audit proof.
Speaker Note: "Current portals waste hundreds of officer hours sorting spam and fake photos. There is no automated fraud defense or instant ward routing."
The Solution

Autonomous, Real-Time Civic Intelligence

An end-to-end cognitive shield and automated dispatch platform

🔍 Instant AI Verification
Sub-second validation of real civic hazards with automated severity scoring (P1 Urgent to P4 Low).
📍 Auto Ward Geo-Routing
Browser GPS + Leaflet GIS instantly binds complaints to specific ward control rooms (e.g. K-West, H-West).
📸 Proof of Resolution
Requires authentic camera photo proof before contractors or officers can close tickets.
Speaker Note: "KAISER AI automates the entire lifecycle: from instant photo verification to ward dispatch and tamper-proof resolution proof."
